02 / ABOUT

ABOUT
ME.

Welcome! I'm Cels, and if you've made it this far, it means something in this portfolio caught your attention. Thanks for keeping on scrolling! ;)

I'm currently in my final year of Advertising and Public Relations at the University of Girona (UdG), where I've been making the most of every academic and hands-on opportunity to develop my creativity and critical thinking. Along the way, I've learned to look at audiences, campaigns and creative projects from different perspectives.

Before starting university, I completed a Higher Vocational Diploma in Audiovisual Production and Live Events at CIFOG. That's where I discovered a whole range of graphic and audiovisual tools that have since become an essential part of how I approach creative projects.

I'd describe myself as a curious, creative and highly organised person. And I guess my schedule proves it! Besides studying, I juggle quite a few other things throughout the week, from coaching and choreographing artistic roller skating to managing the front-of-house team at large weddings.

But hey, I won't bore you with my entire life story… You can find more about all that in my CV on the last page, in case you feel like taking a look. Hehe.

Languages and travelling also have a special place in my heart. I know, I know… sounds like a cliché. But I genuinely believe that being open to the world and exploring different cultures is essential in the creative industry.

In fact, last year I spent six months on an academic exchange in Rio de Janeiro. And yes, the beaches were amazing, but the real highlight was improving my Portuguese and learning to develop campaigns and creative projects for international audiences. That experience gave me new ways of understanding people, communication and the world around me.

I'm especially interested in exploring how brands build their identities, tell meaningful stories and create something that leaves a lasting impression.

Anyway, I'll let you get back to exploring! I hope you enjoy discovering some of my work — and hopefully, there will be plenty more (and even better projects) to come.

I'm still learning, growing and figuring things out. And if there's one thing I'm sure of, it's that I hope to keep doing exactly that for the rest of my life.
